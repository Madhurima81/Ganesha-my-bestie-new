🎨 Header Design Options

Goal: Choose the best header design for your game scenes!
Current Status: UnifiedHeaderV2 exists but we can improve the design
Consider: Child UX, Parent trust, Developer ease
Option 1
⭐ Kid Favorite
Soft Pill Banner (Recommended)
A rounded, pill-shaped header with zone colors, sitting at the top center. Features task text with star progress below. Clean, modern, and playful.
Find Mooshika under the mounds!
⭐ ⭐ ⭐

✅ Pros

  • Very child-friendly
  • Clear visual hierarchy
  • 3D sticker feel with shadow
  • Zone colors prominent
  • Stars easy to see

❌ Cons

  • Takes more vertical space
  • Shadow may clash with some backgrounds

👶 Kid's View:

"Big and colorful! I can see what to do and my stars!"

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent's View:

"Clear task description helps my child stay focused. I can glance and see their progress."

💻 Developer's View:

"Uses CSS variables, easy to implement. Clean separation of content and style. Responsive-friendly."

⭐ RECOMMENDED - Best balance of UX and aesthetics

Option 3
💻 Dev Efficient
Floating Chip (Minimal)
Compact horizontal chip with icon, task, and stars in one line. Minimal space usage, modern material design aesthetic.
🪷
Open the lotus flowers
⭐ ⭐ ⭐

✅ Pros

  • Minimal space usage
  • Modern, sleek design
  • Fast to render
  • Works on mobile

❌ Cons

  • May be too small for young kids
  • Less prominent zone colors
  • Can feel cramped
  • Task text may truncate

👶 Kid's View:

"It's small. I have to look hard to see my stars."

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent's View:

"Looks nice but maybe too compact? I prefer seeing progress more clearly."

💻 Developer's View:

"Very lightweight. Flexbox layout, easy responsive adjustments. Quick to implement."

Option 4
🎭 Most Playful
Story Bubble (Character-Driven)
Speech bubble from a character (Ganesha/Mooshika). Creates narrative feel, like a character is guiding the child.
🐘
"Let's collect the sweet modaks together!"
⭐ ⭐ ⭐

✅ Pros

  • Most engaging/fun
  • Creates story narrative
  • Character-driven
  • Kids love speech bubbles

❌ Cons

  • More complex to style
  • Tail may overlap content
  • Takes most space
  • Can look cluttered

👶 Kid's View:

"Ganesha is talking to me! I love when he tells me what to do!"

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent's View:

"Cute and engaging! But might be distracting if overused. Good for story-heavy scenes."

💻 Developer's View:

"More CSS for the tail. Need to handle character positioning. Medium complexity."

🏆 Winner: Option 1 - Soft Pill Banner

Why? Best balance of child appeal, clarity, and zone color visibility. The 3D sticker effect makes it feel like a playful game element rather than boring UI. Zone colors are prominent, stars are clear, and it's easy to read.

Alternative: Use Option 4 (Story Bubble) for story-heavy scenes like intro/completion!
