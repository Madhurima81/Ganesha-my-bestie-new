## Suspected Cluster Check

### Retaliatory impulse -> de-escalation
- `SIT011`
- Conclusion: not a genuine new mechanism.
- Why: `V2-10 Threshold Choice` can represent the engine without distortion because the causal core is a live retaliate/de-escalate fork.

### Fear / memory replay after the event
- `SIT030`
- Conclusion: genuine gap.
- Why: the danger event is past, but the story's engine is the replaying afterimage and re-separation of memory from present reality.

### Compassionate action toward another
- `SIT055`
- Conclusion: not a genuine new mechanism.
- Why: `V2-10 Threshold Choice` can carry the decisive "step in or stay passive" engine. `V2-06` would over-specify receive/give symmetry that the situation itself does not require.

### Intention vs impact / responsibility / repair
- `SIT060`, with `SIT139` as a nearby cousin
- Conclusion: not a genuine new mechanism.
- Why: `SIT060` fits `V2-09` because the engine is moral refinement from self-defending intention toward accountable repair; `SIT139` fits `V2-02` because it is an escalating avoidance loop broken by confession.

### Imperfection or criticism -> growth
- `SIT073`, `SIT076`
- Conclusion: genuine gap.
- Why: neither story is fundamentally about repeated pressure, threshold choice, inversion, or role reversal. Their causal engine is converting imperfection/criticism from identity verdict into practice-based growth.

### Comparison / scarcity / attention deprivation -> enoughness
- `SIT083`, `SIT089`, `SIT127`
- Conclusion: genuine gap.
- Why: these are not lost-object attention-shift stories, because the lack does not get "returned" or resolved. The engine is from perceived lack/replacement to enoughness, gratitude, or secure belonging.

### Endings / transitions -> continuity
- `SIT120`, `SIT130`
- Conclusion: genuine gap.
- Why: both center disruption or ending of a familiar chapter, then re-finding continuity beyond the break. Existing V2s can imitate parts of this, but none own transition itself as the engine.

### SIT103
- Best existing V2: `V2-12 Quiet Strength`
- Ruling: belongs to existing V2; no separate treatment needed.
- Why: force-through fails, quieter rest/regulation works. That is exactly the quiet-strength causal movement.

### SIT129
- Best existing V2: `V2-12 Quiet Strength`
- Ruling: belongs to existing V2; no separate treatment needed.
- Why: the story turns when nighttime discomfort is met through quiet grounding and trusted comfort rather than through bigger stimulation or escape.

## Smallest Defensible New-Mechanism Set

### 1. V2-N1 - Afterimage Reassurance
Core engine:
- A disturbing event is already over.
- Its mental replay keeps acting like present danger.
- The protagonist learns to distinguish remembered fear from current reality and regains safety through grounding/comfort.

Assigned situations:
- `SIT030`

### 2. V2-N2 - Practice Through Imperfection
Core engine:
- The child meets a painful gap between ideal and actual performance, or between proud effort and corrective feedback.
- The false move is treating imperfection as identity verdict.
- The realization is that practice, revision, and feedback are how growth happens.

Assigned situations:
- `SIT073`
- `SIT076`

### 3. V2-N3 - Enoughness Under Lack
Core engine:
- Another person's possession, attention, or privilege makes the protagonist feel diminished or replaced.
- The false move is treating external scarcity as proof of personal lack.
- The realization is enoughness: gratitude, belonging, and worth are not erased by someone else's having more or receiving more attention.

Assigned situations:
- `SIT083`
- `SIT089`
- `SIT127`

### 4. V2-N4 - Chapter Shift
Core engine:
- A familiar routine, season, or beloved activity breaks or ends.
- The false move is treating the break as total loss.
- The realization is continuity: something real continues across the ending, and the child can step into the next chapter.

Assigned situations:
- `SIT120`
- `SIT130`

## Final Count

**Number of genuinely required new mechanisms: 4**

**Proposed names + situations assigned:**
- `V2-N1 Afterimage Reassurance` - `SIT030`
- `V2-N2 Practice Through Imperfection` - `SIT073, SIT076`
- `V2-N3 Enoughness Under Lack` - `SIT083, SIT089, SIT127`
- `V2-N4 Chapter Shift` - `SIT120, SIT130`
