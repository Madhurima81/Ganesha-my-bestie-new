Recorded voice checklist

Run this before recordings go into more scenes. Tick each device separately. The iPad runs the full list; Android and desktop get the short version. Ticks are saved in this browser only.

Devices: iPad browser (Safari or Chrome: same engine, test either), iPad installed app, Android browser (Chrome), Android installed app, and Desktop (browser or installed app). The iPad is the strictest.

0. Before you start

Every device.

Fresh copy of the newest staging build, device not on silent
Private tab, or close and reopen the installed app twice. iPad/iPhone ringer off silent, volume up, no Bluetooth speaker.

1. Does the voice play? (Modak scene)

iPad first. If the iPad plays everything, the others almost always do.

Opening line plays by itself on a cold start
Tap in from the map; "Mooshika has a journey ahead..." speaks within about 2 seconds.
Later lines in the chain all play
The line after the first one, and the one after that.
Idle hint plays after about 15 seconds of doing nothing
Hands off. "Press and hold Mooshika." should speak, then the second hint.
Play Again plays every line from the start
Garland and flower mini-games use the recordings
Not the computer voice.

2. Interruptions

Where voice breaks most.

Open the symbol sidebar mid-line, then close it
Voice pauses; only the interrupted line replays.
Switch to another app or tab mid-line, then come back
No stuck voice, no overlap.
Lock the screen mid-line, then unlock
A notification or call sound arrives mid-line
Voice resumes or the scene carries on; it does not hang.

3. Mute and network

Sound effects and ambience off in Settings: voice still plays
Slow connection: a line may be late but never hangs or skips the next one
Offline after one load: do the recordings play?
Write what happens in Notes (not yet confirmed they are saved offline).

4. Quality

Listen as a parent and as a 6-year-old.

One consistent voice, no clipped starts or ends, no clicks between lines
Clips 1 to 33 and 34 to 45 were made with different voice settings; note any that stand out.
Nothing speaks in the computer voice
If you hear it, note which line (it has no recording yet).

5. If a line is silent

Collect this before telling anyone. Do not guess.

Note which line, which scene and what you did just before
Write it in Notes below. If you can, read window.__voClipLog (NotAllowedError = still locked).

6. Before adding recordings to another scene

Once per scene.

Every line is in voiceover/script.csv and the clip map with the exact same text, and the file is in vo-manifest.json
No computer-voice calls left in the scene folder
Search for SpeechSynthesisUtterance and speechSynthesis.speak.
Run sections 1 and 2 for that scene on the iPad browser and app first
Then Android and desktop only if the iPad is clean.

Notes

Which line, which device, what you did just before, and anything from window.__voClipLog.
