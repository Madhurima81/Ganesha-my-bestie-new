# Scene Flow — Ganesha My Bestie

This document provides production-ready scene flow for all 22 scenes.
Format includes: opening modal, child actions, in-between modal content, symbols, learning goal, and completion modal.

---

## Zone 1 — Symbol Mountain (Scenes 1–4)

### Scene 01 — Modak

**Opening Modal**
- Title: `Share the Modaks`
- Description: `Mooshika is nearby. Help him find the sweet modaks.`
- Button: `Let's Explore`
- VO: `Mooshika is nearby... Let's find the sweet modaks together.`

**Gameplay Flow**
1. Child taps one of 5 mud mounds to find Mooshika.
2. On correct mound, Mooshika appears with sparkle celebration.
3. Child taps 3 modaks and places/collects them into basket.
4. Child drags modaks to Ganesha.
5. Belly symbol glows and sharing moment completes.

**In-between Modal**
- Title: `You Found the Sweetness!`
- Description: `Now let's share the modaks with love.`
- Button: `Feed Ganesha`

**Symbols Revealed**
- Mooshika
- Modak
- Big Belly

**Learning Goal**
- Focus, sharing, and emotional calm.

**Completion Modal**
- Title: `You Shared the Modaks!`
- Description: `Mooshika helped you find the sweetness.`
- Button: `Continue the Journey`

## Zone 3 — Shloka River (Scenes 10–14)

### Scene 10 — Vakratunda Grove

**Opening Modal**
- Title: `Grow the Lotus and the Tree`
- Description: `Help the lotus bloom and the banyan grow.`
- Button: `Let's Grow`

**Gameplay Flow (Vakratunda phase)**
1. Round 1 (`va`, `kra`):
- Child taps elephant 1.
- `Tap to Say` modal appears for `va`.
- Child repeats; feedback appears.
- Child taps elephant 2.
- `Tap to Say` modal appears for `kra`.
- Child repeats; feedback appears.
- `Vakra` chunk reinforced; lotus advances.
2. Round 2 (`tun`, `da`):
- Same tap -> modal -> speak -> feedback loop.
- `Tunda` chunk reinforced; lotus advances.
3. Round 3 (`vakra`, `tunda`):
- Same loop using chunks.
- Full word `Vakratunda` is reinforced.
- Lotus fully blooms and symbol reveal triggers.

**In-between Modal (repeated after each tap)**
- Title: `Tap to Say`
- Description: `Say: [syllable/chunk]`
- Button: `I Said It`
- Feedback states: `Great pronunciation!` / `Nice try, let's repeat together.`

**Transition Modal (to Mahakaya)**
- Title: `Vakratunda Mastered`
- Description: `Now use your strength power to grow the banyan.`
- Button: `Start Mahakaya`

**Gameplay Flow (Mahakaya phase)**
1. Round 1 (`ma`, `ha`) tap-say-feedback loop.
2. Round 2 (`ka`, `ya`) tap-say-feedback loop.
3. Round 3 (`maha`, `kaya`) chunk tap-say-feedback loop.
4. Full `Mahakaya` reinforcement.
5. Banyan grows to full form.

**Symbols Revealed**
- Vakratunda
- Mahakaya

**Learning Goal**
- Pronunciation confidence, sequencing, adaptability, and inner strength.

**Completion Modal**
- Title: `The Lotus and Tree Shine`
- Description: `You helped the lotus bloom and tree grow strong.`
- Button: `Continue the Journey ????`
