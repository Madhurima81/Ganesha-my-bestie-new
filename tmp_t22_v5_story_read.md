## SIT083 — "Friend got a new toy"

**Page 1:** Gauri sees a friend bring a brand-new toy to school, and everyone gathers around to see it. The new toy comes out of the box, and everyone leans closer. 

**Page 2:** Gauri wanted to have the toy or feel just as excited about what Gauri has. At first the tempting thought still whispered that I need what others have to be happy. 

**Page 3:** Gauri looks at their own toy in their bag and suddenly it doesn't seem nearly as special. The new toy belongs to the friend. 

**Page 4:** Gauri suddenly compares their own favourite things with what someone else has. Gauri reached into their own bag and looked at their own favourite thing for a second, because the crowd around the new toy wasn't going anywhere. 

**Page 5:** Gauri walked over anyway and asked, "Can I see it?". Their friend showed them how it worked, and Gauri held their own thing up right back — for a second, there were two things worth looking at, and Gauri smiled.


**Compression:** Gauri sees a friend bring a brand-new toy to school, and everyone gathers around to see it, while the old thought said I need what others have to be happy. Gauri kept comparing their own things to it, until Gauri understood that Gratitude brings more joy than comparison. Gauri joined in and shared their own thing too, calmer about it than before.

## SIT139 — "Broke something by mistake and hid it"

**Page 1:** Kavi accidentally breaks something important and hides what happened instead of telling the truth. The sound of the crash still seems to hang in the room. Kavi wanted to avoid getting into trouble. 

**Page 2:** At first the tempting thought still whispered that Hiding mistakes makes them disappear. Kavi looks at the broken pieces, quickly moves them out of sight, and hopes nobody asks what happened. 

**Page 3:** The broken object will eventually be noticed. Kavi gets temporary relief from hiding the mistake, but the worry grows.


**Page 4:** Kavi looked at the broken pieces one more time and stopped trying to hide them. Kavi picked them up and went to go find someone, before anyone could ask what happened. 

**Page 5:** Kavi held the pieces out and said, "I broke it. It was me." The relief of saying it out loud was bigger than the worry had been.


**Compression:** Kavi accidentally breaks something important and hides what happened instead of telling the truth, while the old thought said Hiding mistakes makes them disappear. Hiding it only made the worry grow, until Kavi understood that Taking responsibility helps rebuild trust. Kavi told the truth instead, and the relief was bigger than the worry had been.

## SIT148 — "Found something and doesn't know whether to keep it"

**Page 1:** Ved finds something valuable or interesting that clearly belongs to someone else. Ved spots something near the bench and picks it up. Ved wanted to decide what to do with it. 

**Page 2:** At first the tempting thought still whispered that If I found it, it belongs to me. Nobody seems to have noticed. 

**Page 3:** Keeping it would be tempting, but Ved knows it belongs to another person. Ved turns it over in their hands and wonders who lost it.


**Page 4:** Ved stopped turning it over and just held it still. Ved decided not to keep the found item. 

**Page 5:** Ved carried the found item to where its owner would actually find it. "There you go," Ved said, and walked off, easier in their steps.


**Compression:** Ved finds something valuable or interesting that clearly belongs to someone else, while the old thought said If I found it, it belongs to me. Ved turned the found item over, wondering who it belonged to, until Ved understood that Doing the right thing matters more than getting something extra. Ved returned it, and walked off lighter.

## SIT154 — "Friend copies their work or idea"

**Page 1:** Vani shares an idea for a drawing, project, game, or creation, and a friend uses the same idea. Vani looks at the friend's project and stops. Vani wanted to have their own idea recognised as theirs. 

**Page 2:** At first the tempting thought still whispered that If someone copies me, I've lost what makes me special. The idea is almost exactly the one Vani had described earlier. The friend has already used the idea and may not realise why Vani is upset. 

**Page 3:** Vani feels both angry about the copying and uncertain about whether speaking up will hurt the friendship. Vani watched the presentation finish, and stayed quiet for a second, because saying nothing wasn't going to feel any better than speaking up. Vani walked over as soon as there was a chance to talk. 

**Page 4:** "That was actually my idea," Vani said. "I told you about it last week." 

**Page 5:** Their friend's face changed — surprised, then sorry. Vani felt steadier for having said it.


**Compression:** Vani shares an idea for a drawing, project, game, or creation, and a friend uses the same idea, while the old thought said If someone copies me, I've lost what makes me special. Vani almost stayed quiet about it, until Vani understood that My ideas and creativity remain valuable. Vani spoke up about it, and felt steadier for having said it.
