## SIT040 — "Fear of getting in trouble with teacher"

**Page 1:** Tara realizes they made a mistake at school and worries the teacher will be angry. Tara looks at the mess on the desk. Tara wanted to avoid getting into trouble. 

**Page 2:** Nothing about it budged: Tara must decide whether to hide the mistake or tell the truth. Already, the fear had a shape: Making mistakes makes me a bad child. 

**Page 3:** Tara braced for anger, already deciding the mistake meant something bad about who Tara was. Tara paused, then told Their teacher what actually happened, because hiding it wasn't going to work anyway. 

**Page 4:** Their teacher just said, "Thanks for telling me. Let's fix it.". Fear of being seen as a 'bad child' competes with the need to be honest — but the reaction Tara was so sure would come never actually happened. 

**Page 5:** Honesty and responsibility are more important than being perfect. Tara and Their teacher sorted it out together, already moving on, calmer for it.


**Compression:** Tara realizes they made a mistake at school and worries the teacher will be angry, and the old thought said Making mistakes makes me a bad child. Tara told the truth instead of hiding it, until Tara understood that Honesty and responsibility are more important than being perfect. Tara and the moment settled, calmer for it.
