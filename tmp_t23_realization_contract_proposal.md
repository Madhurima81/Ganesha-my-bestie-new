## 1. Audit: what T23 situations actually look like

Real `storySeed`/`ontology` for the 6 active T23 situations: SIT042, SIT086, SIT089, SIT123, SIT158, SIT133.

| Situation | False belief | True belief | What's actually happening? |
|---|---|---|---|
| SIT042 — friend says no to playing, no reason given | If my friend chooses someone else, I'm not important | Friendships can grow even when people spend time with others | A flat "not today," unexplained. Nobody corrects Kavi's read — there is no reveal conversation at all. |
| SIT086 — parent takes a work call mid-news | If I have to wait, what I want to say isn't important | My thoughts matter, and I can wait for the right moment | Parent is legitimately, temporarily unavailable — not dismissive. No misunderstanding to correct, just a wait to get through. |

| SIT158 — friend apologizes after hurting Kavi | Holding on to hurt keeps me safe | Forgiveness helps relationships heal and grow | The other person *does* actively re-engage (an apology) — closest of the six to a two-person beat — but the work is Kavi deciding how to respond, not being corrected about a misread. |
| SIT133 — dared to steal | I must follow my friends to belong | Doing what's right is more important than fitting in | No misjudged person at all. It's an internal temptation/peer-pressure moment; nobody reveals anything to Kavi. |

**Key finding:** The current fixed template (ENCOUNTER → braced for something → INITIAL_RESPONSE → **REVEAL** where the other character explicitly explains what was really going on → DEEPER_NOTICE → CHANGED_RESPONSE → RESOLUTION) assumes T23 is about *misjudging a person's intent, corrected when they explain themselves*. None of the 6 real situations have that shape. Forcing a spoken "explanation" onto SIT042/SIT086/SIT089/SIT123/SIT133 would mean inventing dialogue and plot beats the situation data doesn't support — exactly the kind of decorative invention we've ruled out since T22.

What the six situations actually share: **something outside Kavi's control changes or withholds — attention, closeness, an answer — and Kavi's task is to stop reading that change as a verdict on their own worth**, then to act from the truer read instead. REVEAL, where it survives at all, has to mean "what's actually true becomes clear to Kavi" — not "someone tells Kavi the truth." That's a structural reframe of the beat's *meaning*, not a rewrite of the locked 6-beat sequence itself.

## 2. Four mechanism families (from the actual data)

1. **UNEXPLAINED_WITHDRAWAL** (SIT042, SIT123) — someone Kavi is close to pulls back or changes, with no explanation offered or needed. Kavi's own assumption ("this means I'm not wanted / I have to change too") is the only thing that needs correcting — nobody else corrects it. REVEAL = Kavi noticing evidence that the relationship hasn't actually ended, without anyone saying so.
2. **DIVIDED_ATTENTION** (SIT086, SIT089) — someone's attention is legitimately, temporarily elsewhere for an ordinary reason (a call, a new baby). Kavi reads the delay as being overlooked/replaced. REVEAL = a small, concrete sign that Kavi is still held in mind even during the wait — not a speech about it.
3. **OFFERED_REPAIR** (SIT158) — the other person actively re-engages (apologizes). This is the one family where a second character does something toward Kavi. REVEAL = recognizing that accepting repair doesn't require pretending the hurt didn't happen — Kavi sets the pace, not the friend.
4. **PEER_PRESSURE_TEMPTATION** (SIT133) — no other person needs correcting at all; the pull is internal, toward doing something Kavi knows is wrong to keep belonging. REVEAL = the moment Kavi notices *why* it still feels wrong even with a friend urging it on.

None of these reuse T16's evidence-based belief reassessment, T22's object-discovery, or T21's disrupted-plan-and-adapt shape. All four are genuinely about **something Kavi can't control shifting the ground under a relationship or a choice, and Kavi finding solid footing without needing anyone else to hand them the answer** — but the mechanism differs meaningfully (silent withdrawal / divided attention / offered repair / internal temptation).

## 3. Locked contract (approved 2026-08-11, with correction)

**T23 = realization through self-reinterpretation after an interpersonal/emotional shift, without requiring an explanatory reveal.** Six-beat skeleton frozen. Only REVEAL's semantic function is specialized by mechanism, and it's now sharply defined:

- **REVEAL** = Kavi encounters a **concrete moment** that weakens the original interpretation. Just the fact, no meaning attached yet.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** = Kavi draws the **actual realization/connection** from that fact. This is where the reinterpretation happens — REVEAL only supplies the evidence.

Correction applied to SIT123: the friend's laugh in the original RESOLUTION sketch made the friendship's security depend on the friend's validation — wrong per the earlier T21 lesson (an outcome outside Kavi's control shouldn't be the payoff). RESOLUTION must be **Kavi's own behavior/identity**, not confirmation from the other person.

## 4. Five representative situations for the contract (event-level sketches, revised)

Beats stay structurally fixed: ENCOUNTER / INITIAL_RESPONSE / REVEAL / DEEPER_NOTICE / CHANGED_RESPONSE / RESOLUTION (Phase 7 locked, unchanged). REVEAL is never a person explicitly explaining themselves unless the data shows one (only SIT158 has that). No belief text stated verbatim as narrator explanation.

### SIT042 — UNEXPLAINED_WITHDRAWAL
- **ENCOUNTER**: Kavi asks their friend to play, the way Kavi always asks.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: "Not today," the friend says — no reason given.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: The friend waves before running off — the same wave as any other day.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: "Not today" isn't the same as "not ever."
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi says, "Okay — maybe tomorrow?" instead of walking away certain something is over.
- **RESOLUTION**: Kavi goes and finds something else to do, still counting on tomorrow.

### SIT123 — UNEXPLAINED_WITHDRAWAL
- **ENCOUNTER**: Kavi notices the friend rolling their eyes at the old joke that used to land every time.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: Kavi starts practicing the same eye-roll, the same new words, trying them on before anyone can notice they don't fit.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: The friend still sits right next to Kavi at lunch, same seat as always, cooler words and all.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: Sitting close and sounding different aren't the same thing — the friend never asked Kavi to change.
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi drops the copied voice and tells the joke the old way, the way that's actually Kavi's.
- **RESOLUTION**: Kavi keeps talking the way Kavi always has — whether or not it's the "cool" way doesn't decide it anymore. *(Correction applied: no friend reaction/laugh here — the resolution is Kavi's own behavior, not validation from the friend.)*

### SIT086 — DIVIDED_ATTENTION
- **ENCOUNTER**: Kavi has been holding the news in all afternoon, ready to burst.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: The phone rings, the laptop opens, and the words have to wait.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: Through the doorway, Mama catches Kavi's eye mid-call and mouths, "One minute."
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: Waiting for the right moment isn't the same as being forgotten.
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi goes and does something else instead of standing at the door watching the clock.
- **RESOLUTION**: The second the call ends, Kavi is the first thing Mama turns to — and the news is just as exciting to tell now as it was an hour ago.

### SIT089 — DIVIDED_ATTENTION
- **ENCOUNTER**: Kavi stands by the sofa holding a picture they made, waiting for someone to look up.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: Mama is feeding the baby. Papa is taking photographs. Nobody looks up yet.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: Papa turns the camera toward Kavi too, without being asked.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: There's room in this family for the baby and for Kavi at the same time — it was never one or the other.
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi holds the picture up instead of quietly putting it away.
- **RESOLUTION**: Kavi sits down right there, picture in hand, part of the same photo as the baby.

### SIT158 — OFFERED_REPAIR
- **ENCOUNTER**: The friend who hurt Kavi's feelings comes and stands beside them.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: "I'm sorry," the friend says — the exact words Kavi had been waiting for.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: The hurt is still sitting there in Kavi's chest, even after hearing the apology.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: Still feeling hurt and accepting the apology aren't opposites — Kavi doesn't have to choose one.
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi says, "Thanks for saying that — I'm still a little sad, but I'm glad you did."
- **RESOLUTION**: Kavi sits with the friend anyway, hurt and okay at the same time, on Kavi's own terms.

## 5. SIT133 — PEER_PRESSURE_TEMPTATION (single-example family, kept as sole representative per your note — not generalized further until more situations exist in this family)

There is no other person to correct anything here — the whole beat sequence has to run inside Kavi. REVEAL still has to be a concrete fact, not a stated moral: the truest concrete thing available in this data is Kavi's own hand/body hesitating before any conscious decision is made — the tension is already physical before it's verbal.

- **ENCOUNTER**: "Come on, nobody's looking. Just take it," the friend whispers.
- **INITIAL_RESPONSE**: Kavi's hand drifts toward the small object, half-following before deciding anything.
- **REVEAL** *(concrete fact)*: Kavi's hand stops on its own, hovering, not moving any closer.
- **DEEPER_NOTICE** *(meaning drawn)*: Nobody said the friendship ends if Kavi says no — that part was only ever in Kavi's own head.
- **CHANGED_RESPONSE**: Kavi says, "Nah, I'm good," and steps back from the shelf.
- **RESOLUTION**: Kavi walks out with empty pockets, still standing right there with the friends — belonging never required taking it in the first place. *(No claim about whether the friend approves or reacts — outcome stays inside Kavi's own choice, per the same rule applied to SIT123 and SIT157.)*

## 6. What this deliberately avoids repeating

- No T16 evidence/reassessment, no T22 object-discovery, no T21 disrupted-plan-and-adapt.
- REVEAL is never a person explicitly explaining themselves *unless the situation data shows that* (only SIT158 has one) — everywhere else it's Kavi noticing a concrete fact, not being told a conclusion.
- REVEAL and DEEPER_NOTICE are never blurred: REVEAL is the bare fact, DEEPER_NOTICE is the meaning Kavi draws from it — that split is now explicit in every sketch above.
- RESOLUTION never depends on another character's validation/reaction (fixed in SIT123 and applied up front in SIT133) — the payoff is Kavi's own behavior, something Kavi can actually control.
- No invented dialogue or plot beats beyond what `narrativeSummary`/`storySeed` supports.
- Five genuinely different kinds of "ground shifting" (silent withdrawal / divided attention / offered repair / internal temptation), not one shape with synonym swaps.
