# VO_FLOW.md — Voice-over writing rule for all scenes

The rule every scene VO now follows. Locked for Shloka River (2026-08-29),
Symbol Mountain (2026-08-29), About Me Hut (2026-08-29).

## The four beats

| Beat | What it says |
|---|---|
| **setup** | what's happening + why it matters (the problem / the point of the moment) |
| **mechanic** | one short action sentence — the thing to do, said once |
| **idle** | goal reminder — what's still unsolved, NOT a repeat of the instruction, NOT narration of an animation the child can already see |
| **completion** | what the child discovered or made happen — no new lesson, no generic praise |

Ordering in play: **setup → mechanic → child acts → meaning / completion.**

## What to cut
- Slogans and sugary filler ("Let's make the world smile!", "Keep dreaming!").
- Praise that assumes an experience ("She gives the best hugs.", "best friend").
- Lines that imply a correct emotional answer ("which place feels right").
- Animation narration — if the child can see it, don't say it.
- A second instruction VO chained straight after the first.

## Zone tone (keep the zones distinct)
- **Symbol Mountain** — teaches through symbols. Problem → mechanic → symbol meaning.
- **Shloka River** — teaches through action. Setup = problem + goal; on-screen hint / GestureDemo carries the "how".
- **About Me Hut** — Ganesha and the child getting to know each other. Curious and personal, never instructional or evaluative.

## Scene 19 — Family Tree

| Beat | VO |
|---|---|
| Opening | "Let's meet my family, then make a tree for yours." |
| Ganesha family start | "Tap a circle, then drag each family member to where they belong." |
| Idle | "Who belongs in this spot?" |
| Father / Mother / Brother / Self correct | "Yes — that's my father." / "Yes — that's my mother." / "Yes — that's my brother." / "And that's me!" |
| Father fact | "My father is calm and strong." |
| Mother fact | "My mother is kind and caring." |
| Brother fact | "My brother is brave and rides a peacock." |
| Self fact | "I love modaks and helping my friends." |
| Ganesha tree complete | "You met everyone in my family." |
| Transition | "Now let's make your family tree." |
| Child start | "Tap the people or pets you want to add." |
| Child idle | "Who else belongs on your tree?" |
| Child progress | "Your family tree is growing." |
| Child complete | "These are the people and pets you chose for your family." |
| Scene complete | "Our family trees are different, and each one tells a story." |

Per-spot identifying idle clues kept (`IDLE_HINT_VO`: trident / golden sari / peacock / elephant head) — they name the person, not the mechanic.

## Scene 21 — Dreams & Wishes

| Beat | VO |
|---|---|
| Opening | "I have a few wishes for the world. Will you help me?" |
| Wish 1 setup / mechanic / idle / done | "My first wish is for more kindness." / "Tap the kind actions you can spot." / "Can you find another kind action?" / "You found all the kind choices." |
| Wish 2 setup / mechanic / idle / done | "My next wish is for everyone to have food." / "Drag food to each empty plate." / "There's still an empty plate." / "Now every plate has food." |
| Wish 3 setup / mechanic / idle / done | "My next wish is for a greener world." / "Tap the bare spaces to help the garden grow." / "There's still space for more to grow." / "Look how green it has become." |
| Transition | "Now it's your turn to make a wish." |
| Draw | "Draw something you hope for or dream about." |
| Clouded / idle / reveal | "Your dream is hidden by the clouds. Tap my trunk to clear them." / "There are still clouds in the way." / "There it is — your dream!" |
| Comparison | "Everyone can dream about something different." |
| Completion / ending | "You helped my wishes grow, and made a dream of your own." |

Note: cloud-clearing is a trunk gesture only — no Vakratunda symbolism attached here.

## Scene 22 — My Indian Story

| Beat | VO |
|---|---|
| Opening | "Let's explore some parts of my India story, then you can share yours." |
| Home — Ganesha start | "Move the magnifying glass around the map to find my places." |
| Home — child transition / idle / confirmed | "Now choose a place your family feels connected to." / "Which place feels connected to your family?" / "This is the place you chose." |
| Home — phase complete | "You found some places connected to my story." |
| Language — start / guess / idle | "Tap play and listen carefully." / "Which language do you hear?" / "Listen again, then choose." |
| Language — correct | "Yes — that's Sanskrit, a language used in many mantras." |
| Language — child / idle / confirmed | "Now choose up to three languages you use or hear." / "Choose the languages that are part of your life." / "These are the languages you chose." |
| Festivals — question / idle / correct | "Which festival do I love to celebrate?" / "Look for the festival connected to my favorite sweet." / "Yes — Ganesh Chaturthi." |
| Festivals — child / idle / confirmed | "Now choose the festivals your family celebrates." / "Which celebrations are part of your family?" / "These are the festivals you chose." |
| Completion | "You explored my story, and shared some of your family's story too." |

"languages you use or hear" (not "speak") — a child may understand a family language without speaking it.
