# Ganesha My Bestie - Scene-wise VO Script with Idle Hints

This file is VO-artist ready.
Format per scene:
- `Main VO`: primary narration/instructions
- `Idle Hint VO (Separate)`: nudge lines to play after inactivity

## 1) Beginning Screens VO

### Tap Gate (audio unlock)
- Main VO: `Tap to continue.`
- Idle Hint VO (Separate): `Tap anywhere to begin.`

### Main Welcome Screen
- Main VO: `Hi bestie... I'm Ganesha. Come play with me.`
- Button VO: `Start.`
- Idle Hint VO (Separate): `Tap Start, and we'll begin our adventure.`

### Profile Welcome / Dashboard
- First-time profile VO: `Welcome to the adventure, little explorer.`
- Returning profile VO: `Welcome back! Ready to continue your magical journey?`
- Idle Hint VO (Separate): `Tap Continue Journey to resume, or Explore Map to choose a zone.`

### Map Screen
- First map intro VO: `Tap Symbol Mountain - that's where we start!`
- Idle nudge VO: `Tap Symbol Mountain whenever you are ready!`
- Unlock VO lines:
  - `Look! The Shloka River is flowing!`
  - `Come inside! The About Me Hut is open!`
  - `The cave doors are opening!`
  - `The festival has begun!`

### Zone Welcome Screen (generic)
- Main VO template: `Welcome to {Zone Name}! Tap a card to begin.`
- Zone complete VO template: `You finished {Zone Name}! I'm so proud of you.`
- Idle Hint VO (Separate): `Tap Start to begin, Continue to resume, or Play Again to replay.`

## 3) VO Recording Notes (for artist)
- Tone: warm, calm, encouraging, playful.
- Pace: slow-medium for instructions; slightly celebratory for success lines.
- Idle Hint delivery: short, gentle, non-urgent; avoid sounding corrective.
- Keep small pauses around Sanskrit words so children can repeat.
