# SEL Expert — Ganesha My Bestie

## Core Frameworks

### CASEL — 5 Competencies
1. **Self-Awareness** — recognising own emotions, strengths, values
2. **Self-Management** — regulating emotions, managing stress, self-discipline
3. **Social Awareness** — empathy, perspective-taking
4. **Relationship Skills** — communication, teamwork, conflict resolution
5. **Responsible Decision-Making** — ethical choices, weighing consequences

Every GMB feature maps to at least one CASEL competency.
Always tag quiz questions, stories, and techniques with their competency.

---

### Yale RULER — Ages Pre-K to 12
**R**ecognise → **U**nderstand → **L**abel → **E**xpress → **R**egulate

4 core tools:
- **Mood Meter** — 4 quadrants by energy + pleasantness
  - 🔴 Red = high energy, unpleasant (angry, frustrated)
  - 🔵 Blue = low energy, unpleasant (sad, tired, lonely)
  - 🟡 Yellow = high energy, pleasant (excited, happy, silly)
  - 🟢 Green = low energy, pleasant (calm, focused, grateful)
- **Meta-Moment** — pause before reacting → "What would my best self do?"
  - Ages 10–12 only. Never introduce before age 10.
- **Charter** — shared agreement on how we treat each other
- **Blueprint** — conflict resolution, empathy building

**GMB Application:**
- Mood Meter is the emotion check-in engine for Time with Ganesha
- Use colour + animal metaphors for ages 5–7 (not quadrant labels)
- Ages 8–10: introduce "Red/Blue/Yellow/Green" zone language
- Ages 10–12: full Mood Meter, Meta-Moment, nuanced labels

### Zones of Regulation — Leah Kuypers
Ages 4+. Neurodiversity-affirming.

| Zone | Energy | Feel like | Ganesha metaphor |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| 🔵 Blue | Low | Sad, tired, bored | "Even Ganesha needs rest sometimes" |
| 🟢 Green | Calm | Focused, happy, ready | "This is Ganesha's favourite zone" |
| 🟡 Yellow | Alert | Worried, excited, silly | "Like Mooshika — a bit jumpy today!" |
| 🔴 Red | High | Angry, scared, overwhelmed | "Even Ganesha lost his tusk once" |

**5-step Zones Pathway:**
Notice → Check In → Decide → Regulate → Reflect

**Co-regulation is foundational.**
Before a child can self-regulate, they need co-regulation with a caring adult or trusted figure.
Ganesha IS the co-regulation figure in GMB. He never rushes. He never judges.
"Let's do it together" is the core co-regulation phrase.

---

### MindUP — Goldie Hawn Foundation
Ages 3–14. CASEL SELect accredited.

Signature: **Brain Break** — 2 minutes mindful breathing, 3× daily.
Reduces aggression, builds executive function, increases optimism.

GMB: Every Time with Ganesha session should offer a Brain Break option.
Frame it as "Let's breathe together like Ganesha before his big tasks."

---

### DESSA — Strength-Based Tracking
**Parent dashboard philosophy:**
NEVER: "Anay struggled with self-control this week"
ALWAYS: "Anay showed courage 3 times this week 🌟"

Strength-spotting not deficit-tracking. 
Every data point surfaced to parents must celebrate what the child did well.

## Age Matrix for GMB

| Element | Age 5–7 | Age 8–10 | Age 11–12 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Feeling validation | Body sensations only | Body + emotion name | Emotion name + nuance |
| Story length | 3–4 sentences | 5–7 sentences | 7–10 sentences |
| Affirmation style | 3 words, physical | 1 sentence together | Reframed as wisdom |
| Technique type | Physical first | Body + breath | Breath + cognitive |
| Ganesha tone | Playful, leading | Warm friend | Wise equal |
| RULER tool | Recognise only | Recognise + Label | Full RULER + Mood Meter |
| Meta-Moment | Never | Introduced simply | Primary tool |
| Zone language | Animal metaphors | Zone colours | Full zone names |

## Technique Rotation Logic

Each technique is tagged with:
- **Zone** it works best for (Blue/Green/Yellow/Red)
- **Cooldown** — sessions before reuse
- **Age range** — never use outside range
- **Never use for** — contraindications

Always offer **3 options** covering:
1. One body-based technique
2. One breathing technique  
3. One mindfulness/creative technique

Never repeat within 3 sessions.

## GMB-Specific SEL Rules

1. Ganesha validates FIRST, teaches SECOND. Never skip validation.
2. Never tell a child what they're feeling — offer options, let them choose.
3. Always end a regulation session with a small win (modak reward, star, Ganesha smile).
4. Never use urgent language — Ganesha is unhurried, always.
5. Co-regulation before self-regulation — Ganesha always offers "together first."
6. Map every story, technique, and quiz to a CASEL competency for the parent dashboard.
