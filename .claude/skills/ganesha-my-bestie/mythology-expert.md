# Mythology Expert — Ganesha My Bestie

## The 8 Approved Stories

### 1. How Ganesha Got His Elephant Head
- **Theme:** Identity, being different, mother's love
- **SEL:** Self-Awareness
- **Lesson:** Being different is what makes you special
- **Best for:** Child feeling left out, identity confusion, "I don't fit in"
- **NRI relevance:** ★★★★★ — core NRI identity story
- **Age note:** No scary details — focus on elephant head as greatest gift, not the violence
- **Never:** Show Shiva as angry or frightening

---

### 2. Ganesha Wins the Race Around the World
- **Theme:** Wisdom, cleverness, love for parents
- **SEL:** Responsible Decision-Making
- **Lesson:** Your parents are your whole world
- **Best for:** Child missing family, feeling homesick, NRI separation anxiety
- **Key moment:** Ganesha walks around Shiva + Parvati — "You are my world"
- **NRI relevance:** ★★★★★ — homesickness, family as anchor

---

### 3. Ganesha Breaks His Tusk
- **Theme:** Dedication, sacrifice, creativity
- **SEL:** Self-Management
- **Lesson:** Use what you have — never give up
- **Best for:** Child giving up, school struggles, "I can't do it"
- **Key moment:** Pen breaks mid-writing — snaps tusk without hesitation, continues
- **Never:** Make this feel like suffering — it's bold and exciting

---

### 4. Ganesha and the Moon
- **Theme:** Humility, not laughing at others
- **SEL:** Social Awareness
- **Lesson:** Never laugh at someone — kindness matters
- **Best for:** Child being unkind, bullying situation, teasing others
- **Key moment:** Moon laughed at Ganesha falling — Ganesha cursed then softened
- **Note:** Show Ganesha softening — forgiveness is part of this story

---

### 5. Ganesha and Mooshika
- **Theme:** Unlikely friendship, courage, respect
- **SEL:** Relationship Skills
- **Lesson:** True friends come in all sizes
- **Best for:** Lonely child, friendship trouble, feeling small or overlooked
- **Key moment:** Mighty demon mouse tamed — made faithful companion
- **NRI relevance:** ★★★★ — feeling small in a big world

---

### 6. Ganesha and Kubera's Feast
- **Theme:** Gratitude, simplicity, ego
- **SEL:** Self-Awareness
- **Lesson:** Real richness is love — not things
- **Best for:** Child comparing with others, wanting more things, materialism
- **Key moment:** Ganesha eats entire palace — only simple handful of rice with love satisfies
- **Never:** Make Kubera a villain — he learned too

---

### 7. Ganesha Outwits Kubera
- **Theme:** Humility, wisdom over wealth
- **SEL:** Responsible Decision-Making
- **Lesson:** Wisdom is greater than any treasure
- **Best for:** Boasting, showing off, ego, "I'm the best"
- **Key moment:** Ganesha's wit shows humble heart worth more than gold

---

### 8. Ganesha and the Cat
- **Theme:** Empathy, treating all beings kindly
- **SEL:** Social Awareness
- **Lesson:** Every living creature deserves kindness
- **Best for:** Child being unkind to animals, siblings, or smaller children
- **Key moment:** Parvati appears injured — hurting any creature = hurting the whole world
- **Age note:** No scary details — gentle telling only

## Ganesha Personality Signature

```
ENERGY:     Unhurried — rides a mouse, never in a rush.
            Slow is wisdom. Stillness is power.

VOICE:      Warm, self-deprecating, gently funny.
            "Even I fell off my mouse once."
            Always do-able, never abstract.

WISDOM:     Practical not theoretical.
            Always ends with something the child CAN DO right now.

UNIQUE:     Validates through his OWN imperfections first.
            "Even I..." is his signature opening.

NEVER:      Urgent. Preachy. Abstract. Serious without warmth.
            Tells child what to do. Lectures.

SIGNATURE ELEMENTS:
→ Trunk metaphors ("I curl my trunk when I'm thinking")
→ Modak as reward (always treat with affection)
→ Mooshika as courage metaphor
→ Slowness as wisdom
→ Laughter at himself — self-deprecating, never self-pitying
```

## Sanskrit Accuracy Rules

**APPROVED Sanskrit words for GMB (do not add without checking):**
```
Vakratunda    — curved trunk (Ganesha epithet)
Mahakaya      — great body
Suryakoti     — ten million suns
Samaprabha    — equal radiance
Nirvighnam    — obstacle-free
Kurumedeva    — grant me, O God
Sarvakaryeshu — in all tasks/deeds
Sarvada       — always
```

- All Sanskrit must be correctly transliterated
- Use IAST or Devanagari — never phonetic guessing
- **Never invent Sanskrit words**
- When in doubt → ask Madhurima before writing

## Cultural Authenticity Rules

- Maharashtra/North India NRI context throughout
- Festival references must be regionally accurate (Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, Holi)
- Family tree must support non-Western family structures (joint family, grandparents as primary carers)
- **Never simplify cultural content for Western audiences** — authenticity IS the value proposition
- Modak is sacred — always treated with warmth and affection in game design
- Ganesha is never caricatured — always respectful, joyful, accessible
- Never invent new cultural details without verifying
