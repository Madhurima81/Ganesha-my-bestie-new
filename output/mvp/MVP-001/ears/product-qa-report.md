## Special Tests

### Remove Ganesha Test
Result: `FLAGGED`

Findings:
- The story would still function with a generic listening mentor after one line change.
- The activity becomes a standard SEL sorting exercise if Ganesha is removed.
- The printable still works almost identically without Ganesha because the mechanic itself is not structurally Ganesha-rooted yet.

### Kid Choice Test
Result: `FLAGGED`

Findings:
- The printable feels like schoolwork more than voluntary play.
- The activity is clearer than the printable but still leans instructional.
- The story is understandable, but not especially delightful or adventure-shaped.

## Issues

1. `COMPOSER` High
Try It Today and printable repeat the same mechanic. The slice needs one belief across formats, not one mechanic repeated across formats.

2. `WISDOM` Medium
The wisdom is flattened into generic helpful-vs-mean sorting. The deeper discernment idea is only partially present.

3. `CONTENT` Medium
The story is didactic. It explains the moral clearly but does not yet dramatize it in a memorable way.

4. `ACTIVITY` Medium
The printable feels like a worksheet rather than chosen play for many children.

5. `CONTENT` Medium
The remove-Ganesha test is not passed. Ganesha is named, but not structurally necessary.

6. `RENDERING` Low
No meaningful rendering defects were found. Current issues are product/content issues, not engine failures.

## Baseline

The baseline is promising but not yet strong enough to freeze. The engine works, the Ears belief is coherent, and the printable renders cleanly. The product layer still needs revision because Ganesha specificity is weak and the vertical slice repeats one sorting mechanic instead of building a varied child journey.
