Water Flow — Style Options

Four different ways to render "water moving through the stream" for the Pond scene, side by side. All loop continuously so you can compare motion, not just a single frame.

1. Layered wave bands broad / calm

Two translucent wave shapes scroll sideways at different speeds, overlapping. Reads as a body of moving water rather than individual sparkles — good for a wide, slow stream.

2. Glint streaks current build

Soft horizontal light streaks travel left to right, fading in and out. What's currently wired into the scene now (with stage-aware distance: shorter while flowers block, full length once cleared).

3. Shimmer overlay on stream art subtle

Keep the existing painted stream image as the base "body" of water, and just animate a soft white sheen sweeping across it — the water itself never disappears, it just catches the light.

4. Ripple rings playful

Small circular ripples expand outward from a point and fade — reads more like a droplet/gentle disturbance than directional flow. Nice for the moment water first reaches the lotus, less good for showing continuous movement along a stream.

All four are plain inline SVG + CSS keyframes — no libraries, so any of them drop straight into PondScene.css. Option 2 is the smallest change from what's already live. Option 1 or 3 would read as more "water-like" for a continuous stream; Option 4 works better as a one-off accent (e.g. right when the lotus first receives water) than as the ongoing flow indicator.
