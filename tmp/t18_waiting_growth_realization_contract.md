# T18 Waiting-Growth Branch Contract

Status: active Dev B branch contract for live `T18` coverage.

Family decision:
`T18` belongs to the existing waiting-pattern family under current live selector coverage.

Why:
- Its real natural situations are the same waiting/hurry cases already supporting `T08`.
- The difference is branch emphasis, not mechanism identity.
- `T18` should stress how reaction makes the small problem feel bigger, then reveal the smaller real problem underneath.

Covered now:
- `T18` live natural branch

Audited situations:
- `SIT008`

- `SIT101`
- `SIT108`
- `SIT112`

Branch mechanism:
- a small waiting or hurry frustration appears
- the protagonist reacts against it
- the reaction enlarges the felt problem
- the pattern escalates because it is being fed
- the protagonist pauses
- the “real problem” is revealed to be smaller and more workable than the enlarged version

Pipeline:
`fillStoryTemplate -> buildTemplateRealizationContext -> waiting-pattern family realizer (T18 mode) -> complete story`

Beat responsibilities for `T18`:
- `TINY_PROBLEM`: small real frustration appears
- `IGNORED`: protagonist reacts carelessly instead of understanding it
- `GROWS_1`: reaction enlarges the felt problem
- `GROWS_2`: reaction enlarges it again
- `OVERWHELMING`: the inflated version now feels larger than the original issue
- `PAUSE`: protagonist visibly stops feeding it
- `REAL_PROBLEM`: smaller workable truth becomes visible
- `RESOLUTION`: protagonist addresses the smaller real problem directly

Grounding rules:
- “Growth” must come from the protagonist’s reaction, not from random plot escalation.
- The smaller real problem must be concrete.
- The pause must happen before the true problem is recognized.
- Resolution must solve the smaller real problem on-page.

Prohibited fallbacks:
- No abstract balloon/cloud metaphors unless actually grounded in the situation.
- No copy-paste T08 beat language with only beat IDs swapped.
- No ending where the big problem simply disappears without the smaller truth being named.

QA expectations:
- `postAssertion`: situation details remain concrete.
- `QA-002`: protagonist performs a specific changed action.
- `QA-003`: cause-and-effect from reaction -> growth -> pause -> real problem remains visible.
- `QA-009`: reflective pause visible in manuscript.
- `QA-014`: ending closes emotionally and narratively.
