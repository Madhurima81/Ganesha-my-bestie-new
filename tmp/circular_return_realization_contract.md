# Circular Return Realization Contract

Status: active Dev B family contract for `LOGIC_CIRCULAR_RETURN`.

Purpose:
Realize stories whose proof of change is not a lesson sentence alone, but a return to a similar moment with a different inner stance and outward response.

Covered now:
- `T05` natural pilot branch

Reserved branch:
- `T20` when natural coverage or selector support is ready

Pipeline:
`fillStoryTemplate -> buildTemplateRealizationContext -> circular-return family realizer -> complete story`

Mechanism:
- Opening scene introduces a charged situation.
- Old reaction shows the false belief operating inside that same situation.
- Middle movement creates distance from the opening scene while pressure and perspective shift.
- Insight happens away from the mirrored scene.
- Ending returns to a recognizably similar situation.
- New reaction echoes the opening language or structure while changing the meaning.

Inputs:
- `storySeed.childExperience`
- `storySeed.immediateObstacle`
- `storySeed.emotionalTension`
- `storySeed.narrativeSummary`
- `realizedSituation.sentence`
- `realizedSituation.want`
- `belief.falseBelief`
- `belief.trueBelief`

Beat responsibilities for `T05`:
- `MIRROR_OPENING`: concrete first encounter with the charged situation.
- `OLD_REACTION`: protagonist reacts from the false belief.
- `MIDDLE_JOURNEY`: 2 linked middle beats that pressure and soften the false belief.
- `INSIGHT`: protagonist reaches a new understanding away from the mirrored scene.
- `MIRROR_ENDING`: a deliberately similar situation returns.
- `NEW_REACTION`: protagonist responds differently in language that echoes the opening.

Grounding rules:
- Opening and ending must feel recognizably related, not just thematically similar.
- The mirrored return should use the same social/object/context material when possible.
- The new reaction must be shown through action and felt stance, not just announced.
- Middle movement can generalize slightly, but must stay anchored in the actual situation material.

Prohibited fallbacks:
- No generic ending that abandons the mirror and simply states the moral.
- No “everything felt warmer and freer” filler.
- No fake journey with unrelated obstacle decoration.
- No mirrored ending that changes the external situation so much that comparison disappears.

QA expectations:
- `postAssertion`: opening and ending both still reference the authored situation.
- `QA-002`: new reaction includes meaningful protagonist action.
- `QA-003`: the story still shows how middle pressure leads to insight and changed response.
- `QA-007` and `QA-014`: ending must carry closure and visible change.
