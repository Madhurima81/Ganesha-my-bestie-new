# Phase 7 — Story Director

Version: 1.0
Status: LOCKED

## Purpose

Phase 7 transforms the LOCKED Story Blueprint into a complete Story Plan.

Phase 7 determines HOW the story unfolds.

It does not write final story prose.

## Module Sequence

LOCKED Story Blueprint
        ↓

7B Story Composer
        ↓

        ↓
7D Page Director
        ↓
7E Emotional Director
        ↓
7F Symbol Director
        ↓
7G Craft Director
        ↓
7H Director Validation
        ↓
LOCKED Story Plan

## Inputs

- LOCKED Story Blueprint
- Knowledge libraries
- plannerKnowledge.json
- Story Director rules

## Outputs

- Story Flow
- Scene Plan
- Page Plan
- Emotion Plan
- Emotion Curve
- Symbol Plan
- Symbol Timeline
- Craft Plan
- Craft Strategy
- LOCKED Story Plan
- Director Validation Report

## Phase Boundary

Phase 7 decides the narrative architecture and presentation plan.

Phase 8 writes the story from this plan.

Phase 7 must not invent or silently replace Phase 6 decisions.

## Deliverables

- Story Plan
- Director Validation Report
- LOCKED Story Plan
