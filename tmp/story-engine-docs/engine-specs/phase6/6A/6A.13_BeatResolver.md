# Phase 6A.13 - Beat Resolver

Version: 2.0
Status: LOCKED

## 1. Purpose

The Beat Resolver constructs a coherent Beat Plan by traversing the real Beat state machine.

The real contract is:

```text
6A.11 Logic Family
        ↓
beats.allowed_logic[]
        ↓
Candidate Beats
        ↓
state_change + next_beats
        ↓
Beat Graph / progression
        ↓
6A.13 Beat Plan
```

Meanwhile:

```text
6A.12 Structure
   ↓
core_pattern
scene_flow_template
   ↓
contextual structural guidance
```

So Story Structure does **not** directly select Beats. Instead, the Beat Resolver uses Logic as the hard candidate filter and the Beat graph as the hard progression model, while checking that the resulting progression broadly serves the Structure's authored template.

Beats define the important moments, state changes, and legal transitions the story can contain.

This is the bridge between structural guidance and the later Story Director.

## 6. Responsibilities

The Beat Resolver must:

- Identify candidate Beats whose `allowed_logic[]` includes the selected Logic Family.
- Traverse legal Beat progressions using `next_beats[]`.
- Use `state_change` to maintain coherent narrative progression.
- Respect `repeatable` / `max_repeats` when revisiting a Beat.
- Carry `required_craft[]` codes forward as dependency metadata only.
- Check that the resulting Beat progression broadly serves the selected Structure's authored `core_pattern` and `scene_flow_template`.
- Ensure Mission, Conflict, and Belief Shift are represented through the chosen progression, without pretending they are direct beat-index compatibility fields.
- Provide the progression foundation for Opening, Ending, and later Story Direction.

## 7. Workflow

```text
Read Logic
      ↓
Get candidate Beats whose allowed_logic includes the selected Logic
      ↓
Read selected Structure's core_pattern + scene_flow_template as contextual guidance
      ↓
Choose starting Beat(s) from the candidate set
      ↓
Traverse next_beats graph
      ↓
Track state_change progression
      ↓
Respect repeatable / max_repeats
      ↓
Carry required_craft codes as unresolved dependencies
      ↓
Validate that the progression is legal and broadly serves the Structure template
      ↓
Store Beat Plan
```

## 8. Rules

BR-001 Every selected Beat must exist in the approved Beat index.

BR-002 Beats must be selected from records whose `allowed_logic[]` includes the selected Logic Family.

BR-003 Beat order must follow legal `next_beats[]` graph progression.

BR-004 `state_change` progression must remain coherent across the Beat Plan.

BR-005 `repeatable` and `max_repeats` must be respected.

BR-006 Story Structure's `core_pattern` and `scene_flow_template` are contextual guidance, not direct Beat selectors.

BR-007 Do not invent a hidden `structure -> beat` mapping inside this resolver.

BR-008 Mission, Story Conflict, and Belief Shift should be represented through the chosen Beat progression, but not treated as direct Beat-index compatibility fields unless the source data actually contains such fields.

BR-009 `required_craft[]` codes may be carried as dependencies, but their meaning must not be validated here because the Craft source remains unresolved.

BR-010 The protagonist must remain the agent of meaningful actions.

BR-011 Do not write prose.

BR-012 Do not determine final page placement.

BR-013 Do not invent Beat IDs. Real Beat IDs are `B_SAN_01`-style.

BR-014 Do not resolve Opening or Ending details that belong to their dedicated resolvers.

## 9. Validation

Validate:

- Beat IDs exist.
- Every selected Beat allows the selected Logic Family.
- Beat order is legal according to `next_beats[]`.
- Repeat counts do not exceed `max_repeats`.
- `state_change` progression is coherent.
- `required_craft[]` dependencies are preserved as raw CR codes where present.
- The resulting progression broadly serves the selected Structure's `core_pattern` / `scene_flow_template`.
- Mission, Conflict, and Belief Shift are represented at the level of progression intent, not fabricated Beat-index filters.
- No contradictory Beat dependencies exist.
- Protagonist agency is preserved.

## 10. Failure Handling

If no candidate Beat exists for the selected Logic Family:

- Return `BEAT_UNRESOLVED`.
- Identify the missing coverage as `NO_BEATS_FOR_LOGIC_FAMILY`.
- Do not invent a Beat.

If traversal reaches a dead end before a coherent progression is formed:

- Return `BEAT_UNRESOLVED`.
- Identify the blocking Beat ID and the missing legal continuation.
- Do not jump to a Beat outside `next_beats[]`.

If the Beat graph yields a legal progression that still does not broadly serve the selected Structure template:

- Return `BEAT_UNRESOLVED`.
- Record this as a Structure-guidance mismatch, not as proof of a hidden Structure->Beat compatibility field.
- Do not silently generate a new mapping layer inside the resolver.

If `required_craft[]` contains CR codes:

- Carry them forward unchanged as unresolved dependencies.
- Do not fail solely because their definitions cannot yet be validated.
