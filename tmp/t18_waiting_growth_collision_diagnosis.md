# T18 Waiting/Growth Collision Diagnosis

Date: August 11, 2026

## Audited live natural T18 situations

- `SIT008` Being hurried to put shoes on or leave
- `SIT086` Parent on work call when child wants to share something
- `SIT101` Long wait at doctor, bank, or during travel
- `SIT108` Waiting for a turn on the swings
- `SIT112` Waiting very excitedly for birthday or special package

## Actual mechanism from real situation data

These are not live examples of a free-floating “tiny problem becomes giant” engine.

They are the same **waiting/hurry trigger loop** already proven under `T08`:

- a small frustration appears
- impatience reacts to it
- the reaction makes the whole moment feel bigger
- the same trigger returns
- the protagonist catches the pattern and stops feeding it

## Locked family decision

For current live coverage, `T18` belongs to the **existing waiting-pattern family**.

It is not a new mechanism family on the basis of real selector-supported situations.

## Practical implication

`T18` should be implemented as a **shadow branch** of the waiting-pattern family, with `T18` beat names and slightly different emphasis:

- `T08` emphasis: repeated mistake / pattern noticed / third repetition avoided
- `T18` emphasis: small problem inflated by reaction / pause reveals the smaller real problem

That means:

- no new family contract is needed beyond a T18 branch contract inside the same family
- no ontology-driven rewrite should be attempted
- the current live selector coverage does not justify a wholly separate “growing problem” engine yet

## Risk to avoid

Do not force T18’s prose into abstract “small cloud, bigger cloud, huge cloud” language just because the template label suggests that structure. The real supported situations are concrete waiting-pressure loops, and the family should stay grounded in that.
